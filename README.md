By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Personal Information we collect is used to provide and improve the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

The terms used in this Privacy Policy have the same meanings as in our Terms and Conditions, which are accessible on our website, unless otherwise defined here.

**1. Information Collection and Use**
For a better experience while using our Service, we may require certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

* Name
* Gender (You can select "Not Identified")
* Date of birth
* Delivery Address
* Profile pictures

**Purpose:**

* **Date of Birth:** Ensure compliance with age-restricted products and analyze for tailored promotions.
* **Gender:** Analyze customer demographics and offer personalized promotions and coupons. Users may choose “Not Identified.”

Information collected is retained and used as described.

**Third-party services:** The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to monitor errors and technical issues. Collected data is anonymized and used solely to improve the quality and performance of the app.

**9. Customer Data Platform (CDP) and Analytics**
SOOK Delivery uses a **Customer Data Platform (CDP)** with a unique **Customer ID (UUID linked)** for each account. This data may include:

* Products you view most frequently
* Building or complex you browse orders from
* Search terms used most often
* Menu items or categories you click most

**Key points:**

* Data is **linked to your account** and shared with **Platform Connect X** only to improve SOOK Delivery services.
* Purpose: personalized recommendations, promotions, trend analysis, and app improvement.
* Users **cannot opt-out** of this analytics tracking.

Firebase Crashlytics data is **not linked to users** and is used solely for app stability and performance.
